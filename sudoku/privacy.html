Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This document is available in English only. For questions, contact us at privacy@gnatlabs.com.

This Privacy Policy describes how Gnat Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application uSudoku (the "App").

Summary: uSudoku does not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information. The App uses an anonymous identifier to store your game progress so it can be restored if you reinstall the App on the same device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following limited information:

  • Anonymous identifier: When you first open the App, Firebase (our backend provider) assigns your device a random, anonymous identifier. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • Game data: We store your puzzle progress, statistics (puzzles solved, best times, streaks), and settings, associated with your anonymous identifier.
  • Device information: Basic technical information such as operating system version, used only for app functionality and crash diagnostics.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use This Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Save and restore your game progress, statistics, and settings
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues

3. Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored using Google Firebase, a secure cloud platform, associated only with your anonymous identifier. Game data is also cached locally on your device.

Because no personal information is collected, this data cannot reasonably be linked back to you as an individual.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase: Our backend infrastructure provider, which stores anonymous game data on our behalf (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since uSudoku does not collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal profile to access or correct. You can remove all your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App: This immediately removes all locally stored data from your device
  • Anonymous cloud data: Anonymous game data associated with your device's identifier is automatically deleted after a period of inactivity, or can be removed on request by contacting us

If you are in a region with specific data protection rights (such as the EU/GDPR) and wish to make a request regarding anonymous data associated with your device, please contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information from any user, no personal data is knowingly or unknowingly collected from children.

7. International Data Transfers

Anonymous game data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Google's servers are located.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
