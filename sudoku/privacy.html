Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gnat Labs ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application uSudoku (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name.
  • Authentication data: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Game data: We store your game progress, statistics (puzzles solved, best times, streaks), and settings.
  • Device information: We may collect basic device information such as operating system version for app functionality purposes.
  • Anonymous usage: If you play as a guest, we assign an anonymous identifier to store your local game progress.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Sync your game progress across devices when you are signed in
  • Display your statistics within the App
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • Respond to your requests or questions

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, a secure cloud platform. Data is stored on Google's servers and is protected by Google's security infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information.

Game data stored locally on your device is kept in local storage managed by the operating system.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase: We use Firebase for authentication and data storage. Google's Privacy Policy applies to their services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and cloud database (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — OAuth authentication (Google Privacy Policy)

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data. You can do this directly in the App via Account → Delete Account, or by contacting us.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal data

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it longer.

Local data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Google's servers are located. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
